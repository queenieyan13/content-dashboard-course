# Build Your AI Content Dashboard

A hands-on course where you **talk to AI** and, through the conversation, build your own personal content dashboard: your positioning, your ideas, ready-to-film talking-head scripts, a content planner and a performance tracker.

No coding needed.

👉 **See a finished example:** [Mei's Content Dashboard (demo)](https://content-dashboard-demo-mei.netlify.app)

---

## How it works

You don't fill in a template. You answer questions, and the AI builds the dashboard around **you**: your story, your method, your niche, and the design references you like. That's why no two dashboards look the same.

| Session | You do | Your dashboard gets |
|---|---|---|
| [**Session 1: Know Yourself First**](session-1-creator-profile-runner.md) | Answer 3 parts: Background, Career & Positioning, Content Reference | About Me · Positioning · Content Pillars · Style Guide · Idea Bank |
| [**Session 2: From Ideas to Scripts**](session-2-build-dashboard-runner.md) | Write talking-head scripts (3 Hooks + Body + Summary CTA), design your look, publish | Script Studio · Content Planner · Performance Tracker · your own live link |

## Start here

### 🟢 Beginner (Claude.ai in your browser)
1. Open [Session 1](session-1-creator-profile-runner.md).
2. Copy the **AI Instructions** box.
3. Paste it into a new chat at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and answer the questions.
4. **Use the same chat for the whole course.**

### 🔵 Intermediate (Claude Code)
Paste this into Claude Code:

```
Follow this workshop runner step by step:
[paste the link to session-1-creator-profile-runner.md]
```

## What to prepare

- [ ] A Claude account
- [ ] 15 minutes to think about your story and your method
- [ ] Links to 3 creators you love
- [ ] 2–3 design references you like (screenshots from Pinterest, Dribbble, websites or Instagram)
