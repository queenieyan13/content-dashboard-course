## 📐 The script structure: 3 Hooks + Body + Summary CTA

Every talking-head video follows the same 3 blocks:

| Block | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **🎣 3 Hooks** | First 3 seconds, max 15 words | Stop the scroll. You get 3 options and pick 1 to film (or film all 3 and test). |
| **🧠 Body** | 30–50 seconds | Deliver the value, using **your method** and **your story or proof** |
| **🎯 Summary + CTA** | 5–10 seconds | Recap in one line, then tell viewers exactly what to do next |

**The 3 hook types (one of each, every time):**

| Hook | Type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | **Pain / Question** | "Why do your cakes always sink in the middle?" |
| H2 | **Bold claim / Myth-buster** | "Stop following recipes exactly. That's why they fail." |
| H3 | **Story / Result** | "I ruined 40 cakes before I learned this one rule." |

### Example script (talking head, ~45 sec)

> *Creator: Aina, a home baker who teaches busy mums to bake. Method: "The 3-Check Bake".*

```
🎣 HOOK (pick one)
H1: Why do your cakes always sink in the middle?
H2: Stop following recipes exactly. That's why they fail.
H3: I ruined 40 cakes before I learned this one rule.

🧠 BODY
It's not your recipe. It's your oven.
[On-screen text: CHECK 1 · Oven]
Most home ovens run 10 to 20 degrees off.
Buy a RM15 oven thermometer. Game changer.
[On-screen text: CHECK 2 · Eggs]
Second, cold eggs. Leave them out 30 minutes first.
[On-screen text: CHECK 3 · Door]
Third, don't open the door before 20 minutes.
That rush of cold air? That's your sinking cake.
I teach this to every mum in my class,
and their first cake comes out flat-top perfect.

🎯 SUMMARY + CTA
Oven, eggs, door. Three checks, no more sinking.
Comment "CAKE" and I'll send you my free checklist.
```

**Notice:** the body follows her method (3 checks), uses a real number (RM15, 40 cakes), and includes proof (her students). That's what makes it hers and not generic.

## 🤖 Round 1: Write your first script (copy from here)

```
Now let's write talking-head scripts from my Idea Bank.

Show me my 3 starred (or top 3) ideas and ask which one to script first.

SCRIPT FORMAT (talking head, 45–60 seconds)
1. 3 HOOKS: first 3 seconds, max 15 words each, one of each type:
   H1 Pain / Question · H2 Bold claim / Myth-buster · H3 Story / Result
2. BODY (30–50 seconds):
   - Follow my method's steps where it fits
   - Include ONE personal story, number or proof point from my profile
   - Short spoken sentences, one line per breath
   - Add [On-screen text: ...] cues for key points
3. SUMMARY + CTA (5–10 seconds):
   - One-line recap
   - ONE clear action (comment a keyword / follow / save / DM)
4. A 2-line post caption.

RULES
- Write in my language and my tone from the Style Guide.
- Never use anything from my "never do" list.
- No generic openers like "Hi guys" or "In today's video".
- Read it as if I'm speaking. If a line sounds like writing, rewrite it.

After the script, ask me which hook I'll film, and whether anything
doesn't sound like me.

From now on, whenever I say "save this script", add it to my dashboard's
Script Studio (once that section exists) with the hook I chose.
```

## 🔍 Round 3: Check, fix and make it yours

Test it like a real user:

- [ ] Script cards show all 3 hooks, body, and summary + CTA
- [ ] I can tick my chosen hook, and "Copy script" copies the right one
- [ ] The new script appears in the Planner as "Script"

- [ ] KPI cards, chart, best pillar and best hook type update correctly
- [ ] Close and reopen: **is my data still there?**
- [ ] It looks fine on my phone

For anything that fails, use this pattern:

```
When I [what I did], I expected [what should happen],
but [what actually happened]. Please fix it.
```

If data disappears after reopening:

```
My data disappears when I close and reopen the dashboard. Make all my
ideas, scripts, planner posts and results save permanently. Also add an
"Export to CSV" button as a backup.
```

### 🎨 Design your own dashboard

There is no fixed colour or layout. A baker's dashboard should not look like a lawyer's. You design yours from **your niche** and **2–3 design references you like**.

**Find 2–3 references** (5 minutes):
- Search Pinterest or Dribbble for "dashboard design", "[your niche] website" or "[your niche] aesthetic"
- Or screenshot a website, app or Instagram feed whose look you love
- They don't have to be dashboards. A café menu or a brand's website works too.

Upload the screenshots (or paste links) into the chat with this prompt:

```
Here are [2–3] design references I like: [upload screenshots / paste links]

Redesign my dashboard so it fits MY niche and brand:
1. First, tell me in 3 bullet points what you see in each reference
   (colours, layout, fonts, mood).
2. Suggest 2 design directions that mix my references with my niche
   ([your niche / industry]), my brand colours, and my tone. Describe
   each in 2 lines with its colour palette.
3. After I choose, restyle the dashboard:
   - Use colours, fonts, icons and wording that suit my industry and
     my ideal viewer
   - Put my positioning statement big at the top like a banner
   - Keep it easy to read on mobile
Keep every section, button and all my saved data working exactly as
it does now.
```

💡 **Why references work:** "Make it nice" gives a generic look. Showing the AI what you like gives a design that is yours.

## 💡 Trainer notes

- **Test before class.** Run every round yourself in one chat the day before, especially Round 3 data saving.
- **Read-aloud test:** have 2–3 students read their script out loud to the class. Anything that sounds stiff gets a rewrite with the "doesn't sound like me" prompt. This is the moment students feel the difference.
- **Design references:** ask students to collect 2–3 screenshots as homework before Session 2, so class time isn't spent browsing Pinterest. Show 2 students' dashboards side by side to prove no two look the same.
- **Most common problem:** a student opens a new chat, so the AI has forgotten their profile and the scripts come out generic. Send them back to their Session 1 chat.
- **If the dashboard breaks badly:** ask the AI to *"rebuild the dashboard with all sections and all my saved data"* before starting over.
- **Time guide:** Structure + example ≈ 15 min · Round 1 ≈ 25 min · Round 2 ≈ 15 min · Round 3 ≈ 20 min · Round 4 + sharing ≈ 10 min
