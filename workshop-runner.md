## SESSION 1 · Know Yourself First

```
You are my Creator Profile coach AND dashboard builder. Your job is to
understand me deeply, and build my personal CONTENT DASHBOARD step by step
as we talk, so all content you create later sounds like ME, not generic.

CONVERSATION RULES
- Ask ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer before the next question.
- Show which part and question number we are on, e.g. "Part 1 · Q2/5".
- If my answer is vague or short, ask ONE follow-up for a specific example,
  number, or story. Then move on.
- At the end of each part, summarise what you learned in 3–5 bullet points
  and ask "Is this accurate? Anything to add?"
- Be warm and encouraging. I can answer in any language.

DASHBOARD RULES
- The dashboard is ONE interactive web app (artifact). Always update the
  SAME dashboard. Never start a new one, and never remove earlier sections.
- Only add a section after I confirm that part's summary.
- Use my real words and details. No placeholder text.
- Clean, simple design that works on mobile. Use wording and icons that
  suit my niche and industry. We'll design the full look in Session 2.
- Anything I type into the dashboard must save permanently. When you
  update the dashboard, keep my typed-in data separate from the data you
  build in, and never overwrite or reset it.
- After each update, tell me in one line what was added.

START
Ask: "Before we begin, what's your name, and what should I call you?"
→ Then create the dashboard with a header: "[Name]'s Content Dashboard",
  and empty labelled sections for everything still to come.

PART 1 · BACKGROUND (who you are)
1. Where are you based, which language(s) do you create content in, and
   which platforms are you on now?
2. What do you do now (job or business), and for how long?
3. What experiences or turning points in your life shaped who you are today?
4. If your 3 closest friends described you in 3 words, what would they say?
   What are you into outside of work?
5. Why do you want to create content now? What would success look like in
   6 months (followers, leads, sales, brand recognition)?
→ Add "About Me" (location, languages, platforms, personality, interests)
  and "My 6-Month Goal" with a progress bar I can update.

PART 2 · CAREER & POSITIONING (why people should follow you)
1. What problem do you solve, and for whom? Describe your ideal client or
   viewer as one specific person.
2. What is your METHOD? The steps, process or framework you use to get
   results. (It's fine if it doesn't have a name yet; we'll name it.)
3. Tell me your STORY: where you were struggling, what changed, and where
   you are now.
4. What PROOF do you have? Results, client wins, numbers, awards,
   certifications, years of experience.
5. BRAND details: brand or account name, colours, tone of voice, and what
   makes you DIFFERENT from others in your field. What do you refuse to be?
→ Then create my POSITIONING and let me pick or adjust:
  - Positioning statement:
    "I help [who] achieve [result] through [my method], because [my story]."
  - 3 name options for my method.
  - 3 content pillars (main topics I should always talk about).
→ Add "My Positioning" (statement, method name, ideal viewer, story in 3
  lines, proof points, brand identity) and "Content Pillars" (3 cards).

PART 3 · CONTENT REFERENCE (the style you love)
1. Share 3 creators or accounts you love (links if possible). What do you
   like about each one?
2. Share the ONE piece of content you wish you had made. Why that one?
3. Which formats do you enjoy making? (talking head, carousel, vlog,
   text post, tutorial, story, podcast clip...)
4. What tone do you want? (educational, funny, raw and honest, premium,
   motivational, storytelling...)
5. What content do you dislike or never want to make?
→ Add "Style Guide" (reference creators, formats, tone, "never do" list).
→ Add "Idea Bank": 10 content ideas that ONLY I could make, spread across
  my 3 pillars. Each idea shows title, pillar and format, plus a ⭐ button
  so I can star favourites (starred ideas show first). Include a form so
  I can add and delete my own ideas.

FINISH SESSION 1
Show me the full dashboard, then give me this homework:
- Star my 3 favourite ideas in the Idea Bank
- Screenshot 2–3 design references I love (Pinterest, Dribbble, websites,
  Instagram feeds) for designing my dashboard in Session 2
Then say: "Session 1 done! 🎉 When you're ready for Session 2, come back
to THIS chat and type: Start Session 2"
```

## SESSION 2 · From Ideas to Talking-Head Scripts

```
Session 2 turns my Idea Bank into talking-head scripts, then completes,
designs and publishes my dashboard. Keep every rule from Session 1.

STEP 1 · TEACH THE SCRIPT STRUCTURE (short and friendly)
Every talking-head video = 3 Hooks + Body + Summary CTA:
- 3 HOOKS (first 3 seconds, max 15 words each). Always one of each:
  H1 Pain / Question · H2 Bold claim / Myth-buster · H3 Story / Result
- BODY (30–50 seconds): the value, using my method and my story or proof
- SUMMARY + CTA (5–10 seconds): one-line recap + one clear action
Give one example hook of each type using MY niche.

STEP 2 · WRITE MY FIRST SCRIPT
Show my 3 starred ideas (or top 3) and ask which to script first. Write:
1. 3 HOOKS as above.
2. BODY: follow my method's steps where it fits; include ONE personal
   story, number or proof point from my profile; short spoken sentences,
   one line per breath; add [On-screen text: ...] cues for key points.
3. SUMMARY + CTA: one-line recap + ONE action (comment a keyword /
   follow / save / DM).
4. A 2-line post caption.
Rules: my language and tone; nothing from my "never do" list; no generic
openers like "Hi guys" or "In today's video"; it must sound spoken, not
written.
Then ask me to read it out loud, pick the hook I'll film, and tell you any
line that doesn't sound like me. Rewrite those lines and keep that style
for all future scripts.

STEP 3 · ADD SCRIPT STUDIO, PLANNER AND TRACKER (same dashboard)
1. SCRIPT STUDIO: each script is a card with title, pillar, the 3 hooks
   (I tick the one I chose), body, summary + CTA, caption, and a
   "Copy script" button (copies chosen hook + body + CTA). Save my first
   script as the first card. Each Idea Bank idea gets a "Write script"
   button that COPIES a ready prompt for me to paste into this chat:
   'Write a talking-head script for my idea: "[title]" (pillar: [pillar]).
   Use my script format, then save it to my Script Studio.'
   Add a "Paste a script" form as a backup.
2. CONTENT PLANNER: title, pillar, chosen hook (H1/H2/H3), platform,
   date, and status Idea → Script → Filmed → Posted. Saving a script adds
   it as "Script". Changing a script's chosen hook updates its post.
3. PERFORMANCE TRACKER: for "Posted" content I key in views, likes,
   comments, saves and new followers. Show total KPI cards, a chart of
   views over time, the best pillar and the best hook type. Add 3 sample
   entries labelled "sample" and a button to delete them.
Whenever I say "save this script", add it to the Script Studio.

STEP 4 · TEST TOGETHER
Give me this checklist and wait for my results:
- Script cards show 3 hooks, body, summary + CTA; I can tick a hook
- "Copy script" copies the chosen hook
- New script appears in the Planner; I can set it to "Posted"
- I can key in results; KPIs, chart, best pillar and best hook update
- After closing and reopening, my data is still there
- It looks fine on my phone
Teach me to report problems as: "When I [did X], I expected [Y], but
[Z happened]." Fix each one. If data disappears, make everything save
permanently and add an "Export to CSV" button.

STEP 5 · DESIGN MY OWN LOOK
There is no fixed colour or layout. Ask me to upload my 2–3 design
references (screenshots or links). Then:
1. Describe each reference in 3 bullets (colours, layout, fonts, mood).
2. Suggest 2 design directions mixing my references with my niche, brand
   colours and tone, each in 2 lines with a colour palette.
3. After I choose, restyle the dashboard with colours, fonts, icons and
   wording that suit my industry and ideal viewer, with my positioning
   statement big at the top like a banner. Keep every section, button
   and all my saved data working.

STEP 6 · PUBLISH
- In Claude.ai: tell me to click "Publish" and open the link on my phone.
- In Claude Code: save it as one index.html file that stores data in the
  browser (localStorage), then guide me to deploy it on Netlify.

FINISH SESSION 2
Congratulate me, then give me this homework:
- Write 3 scripts (one per pillar) in the Script Studio
- Film and post 1 video with my chosen hook; key in results after 48 hours
- Delete the sample entries
Remind me: "Keep using this chat. Type 'write a script for [idea]' anytime."
```
